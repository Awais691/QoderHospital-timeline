Emergency care

Emergency care, every hour of every day.

Our emergency department is staffed 24 hours a day, with imaging, laboratory and critical care teams on site. If you are unsure whether something is an emergency, call us and we will help you decide.

Call 911 for a life-threatening emergency

Examples include chest pain, difficulty breathing, severe bleeding, sudden weakness or confusion, a serious injury, or a baby who is unresponsive.

(555) 014-9111 Find the entrance

Fictional phone number and service details for this demonstration.

When you arrive

A triage nurse will assess how urgent your symptoms are. The most seriously unwell patients are seen first, regardless of arrival time.

Bring what you can

If possible, bring a medication list, identification and details of allergies. Do not delay coming in to collect paperwork.

Ask for an update

Waiting is difficult. A staff member can explain what is happening and what the next step is. Please treat the team with respect.
