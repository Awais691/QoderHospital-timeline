Emergency & urgent care

Call 911 for chest pain, severe difficulty breathing, fainting, sudden weakness or confusion, severe bleeding, a serious injury, or any situation that feels immediately life-threatening. Do not drive yourself if you are seriously unwell.

Yes, in this fictional hospital the emergency department is open 24 hours a day, every day. The sample emergency number is (555) 014-9111.

A nurse assesses the urgency of each patient's condition on arrival. The most seriously unwell are seen first, so waiting times can vary. If your symptoms change while waiting, tell a staff member immediately.

No. Contact forms are not monitored in this demo and would not be appropriate for urgent care in a real service. Call the emergency number or 911 instead.
