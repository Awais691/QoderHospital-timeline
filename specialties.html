01

Cardiology

Heart health from prevention through complex intervention, supported by cardiac imaging and rehabilitation.

  • Coronary artery disease and heart failure
  • Heart rhythm assessment and palpitations
  • Preventive cardiology and cardiac recovery

02

Neurology

Assessment and long-term care for conditions affecting the brain, spinal cord, nerves and muscles.

  • Headache, migraine and facial pain
  • Epilepsy, stroke recovery and movement disorders
  • Memory assessment and cognitive change
Meet the neurologist Request care
03

Orthopedics

Care for bones, joints, muscles and movement — with surgery reserved for when it offers clear benefit.

  • Hip and knee joint replacement
  • Sports injuries and shoulder problems
  • Fracture care and joint preservation
Meet the orthopedic surgeon Request care
04

Pediatrics

Care for children from their first days through adolescence, with parents treated as part of the team.

  • Newborn, infant and well-child checks
  • Childhood asthma and common infections
  • Growth, development and vaccination guidance
Meet the pediatrician Request care
05

Dermatology

Evidence-led care for skin, hair and nail conditions in adults and children.

  • Eczema, psoriasis and chronic skin conditions
  • Acne, rosacea and hair or scalp disorders
  • Skin cancer screening and mole assessment
Meet the dermatologist Request care
06

Women's health

Pregnancy, reproductive health and menopause care, coordinated with midwifery and neonatal teams.

  • Prenatal care and high-risk pregnancy support
  • Menstrual disorders and menopause care
  • Minimally invasive gynecologic procedures
Meet the gynecologist Request care
07

General medicine

A first point of contact for complex symptoms, long-term conditions and coordination between services.

  • Complex medical assessment and second opinions
  • Diabetes, hypertension and respiratory conditions
  • Preventive health and care coordination
Meet the physician Request care
08

Ophthalmology

Eye and vision care for adults and children, from routine checks to cataract and glaucoma management.

  • Cataract assessment and surgery
  • Glaucoma and diabetic eye monitoring
  • Dry eye and pediatric vision concerns
Meet the ophthalmologist Request care
09

Ear, nose & throat

Medical and surgical care for hearing, sinus, throat and voice concerns in adults and children.

  • Sinus surgery and nasal obstruction
  • Hearing loss, tinnitus and ear infections
  • Tonsil, adenoid and voice disorders

Radiology & diagnostics

Imaging and laboratory services that support clinicians across the hospital. Most radiology care is by referral.

  • Ultrasound, CT and MRI
  • Breast imaging and diagnostic screening
  • Image-guided procedures and reporting
