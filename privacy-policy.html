Privacy policy.

Not a real hospital

CareWell Hospital, its staff, patients and services are fictional. This policy describes the demonstration website only and is not legal advice.

The short version

  • This site is static HTML, CSS and JavaScript. It has no server, database or user accounts.
  • Form entries stay in your browser and disappear when you close or reload the page.
  • No analytics, advertising pixels or third-party tracking are used.
  • No cookies or local storage are used for personal information.
  • No real appointments, messages or payments are created.

Who this policy covers

This policy covers the CareWell Hospital demonstration website and its pages. CareWell Hospital is a fictional organisation created for a frontend development project. It is not affiliated with any real healthcare provider, and no real clinical services are offered through this site.

References to “we”, “us” or “the hospital” mean this fictional demonstration. References to “you” mean a visitor to the site.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. The site does not include a backend application, database, login system or server-side form handler. There is no mechanism for information entered into the site to reach a hospital, developer or third party.

Your web browser or static host may process standard technical information to deliver the files, such as your IP address and the pages requested. That processing is determined by the hosting environment you choose to use and is outside the code of this demonstration.

Forms and demo summaries

The appointment and contact forms are demonstrations of form validation and user experience. When you submit a form, JavaScript checks the fields and generates a sample summary in your browser. Nothing is sent over a network by the site itself.

  • Form values remain in the browser's memory for the current page.
  • Reloading or closing the page clears those values.
  • No appointment, enquiry, payment or record is created.
  • Do not enter real medical, financial or identity information into a demonstration form.
If you need real medical care, contact a qualified healthcare provider or emergency service. Do not rely on this demonstration for advice, diagnosis or treatment.

Cookies and browser storage

This site does not set cookies and does not use localStorage, sessionStorage or IndexedDB to store personal information. The only browser storage used by the code is transient JavaScript memory while the page is open.

If you open the site through a hosting provider that adds cookies or analytics, those features would be governed by that provider's policies rather than this demonstration. The project itself does not include them.

Analytics and tracking

No analytics service, advertising network, social media pixel or behavioural tracking is included. The site loads fonts and icons from its own local files and makes no calls to external tracking domains for those assets.

Third-party services and links

The demonstration does not embed third-party maps, videos, chat widgets or tracking libraries. The stylised map is an illustration built with HTML and CSS, not a live mapping service.

Some links may take you to external sites if you choose to follow them. Those sites have their own privacy policies. This demonstration is not responsible for their content or practices.

Security

Because the site is static and does not accept or store personal information, the data security risks usually associated with forms and databases are not present. However, no website can guarantee absolute security. If you deploy this project, use reputable hosting, keep dependencies updated and follow standard security practices.

Children's privacy

This demonstration is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone. As the site does not collect personal information at all, no special children's data process is in place.

Your choices and rights

Because no personal information is collected or retained, there is no stored profile to access, correct, export or delete. You can clear any form values by reloading the page or closing the browser tab.

If you believe a deployment of this project has collected information contrary to this policy, contact the operator of that deployment. This repository itself does not operate a public service.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the demonstration changes. The date at the top of the page shows the most recent revision. Any future version would continue to describe the static, non-collecting nature of the project unless a different implementation is introduced.
